Collector Submissions

Have a U.S. military M1911 or M1911A1? The archive accepts documented examples regardless of condition, originality, rebuild status, finish wear, replacement parts, or completeness.

  • CMP pistols and data sheets
  • Arsenal rebuilds and mixed-part pistols
  • Original finish examples
  • Worn, refinished, incomplete, or damaged examples
  • Rare inspection marks, rebuild stamps, and serial-number observations
  • Museum, auction, provenance, or private-collection records
  • Historical photographs and comparison images

Submit information and photos to:

1911database@gmail.com

By submitting photographs or information, contributors confirm they have permission to share the material for historical reference use.

About This Database

This website is an independent historical and collector reference resource for U.S. military M1911 and M1911A1 pistols.

This site documents examples for historical comparison, including original pistols, arsenal rebuilds, mixed-part pistols, worn examples, refinished examples, incomplete examples, and documented survivors in any condition.

This site is not affiliated with Colt, Ithaca, Remington Rand, Union Switch & Signal, Singer, the U.S. Government, the Department of Defense, CMP, or any firearms manufacturer.

All information is provided for educational, historical, and research purposes only.
