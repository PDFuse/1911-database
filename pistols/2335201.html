Known Correct Features To Verify

Feature Expected / Notes
Frame Colt-manufactured M1911A1 frame within the 1945 serial range.
Slide Correct Colt slide markings should be verified against known late-war Colt examples.
Inspector Mark Verify the correct U.S. military inspection initials and ordnance acceptance markings for the production period.
Finish Late WWII production should generally be evaluated for correct parkerized finish and consistency across frame, slide, and small parts.
Barrel Barrel markings should be photographed and compared to accepted Colt WWII barrel variations.
Mainspring Housing Check for the correct arched, checkered M1911A1-style mainspring housing unless documented as replaced during service or rebuild.
Small Parts Trigger, hammer, safety, slide stop, grips, and pins should be checked for WWII-correct style and finish.
Rebuild Marks Look for arsenal rebuild stamps or replacement-part indicators. Rebuild marks do not automatically make a pistol bad, but they change collector classification.
Matching Condition Evaluate whether slide, frame, barrel, finish, and parts appear consistent with one another or show signs of later replacement.
Research Note: These are verification points, not a final authentication. Use clear photographs, known reference examples, and expert review before assigning originality or value.
