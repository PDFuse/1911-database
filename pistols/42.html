Documentation

Factory letter: The available Colt Archive letter information identifies serial number 42 as a Colt Model 1911 Automatic Pistol in .45 caliber with a 5 inch barrel and blue finish, sold to the United States Government and shipped to the Commanding Officer at Springfield Armory on 04 January 1912 in a shipment of 50 pistols.

Auction observation: Last observed through Rock Island Auction Company. This page summarizes the auction description in short factual form only and does not reproduce auction photography.

Reported Early Features

SlideRight side reported as MODEL OF 1911. U.S. ARMY. Left side described with early patent/address layout and Rampant Colt behind the serrations.
FrameLarge UNITED STATES PROPERTY marking and early No.42 serial-number format reported.
InspectorW.G.P. inspector mark reported in early rotated format.
FinishHigh-polish blue finish with nitre-blue small parts reported.
Early PartsReported early hand-checkered slide stop and thumb safety, dimpled magazine catch, wide checkered hammer, short grip safety, smooth flat mainspring housing with lanyard loop, and double-diamond checkered walnut grips.
MagazineReported as a later two-tone replacement magazine with lanyard-loop base.

Condition Summary

Reported condition was approximately 60% bright original high-polish blue finish with approximately 75% bright original nitre blue on small parts, with remaining areas showing grey patina and scattered light pitting. Grips were described as fine with handling wear, minor dings and scratches, and mostly well-defined checkering. Mechanically functional per listing description.

Research Notes

Collector significance: Serial number 42 is a two-digit Colt and is described as part of the first Colt shipment to Springfield Armory under the U.S. contract.

Verification status: Factory-lettered and auction-observed. Final originality conclusions should still be based on direct inspection of the pistol, markings, barrel, small parts, magazine, and finish.
