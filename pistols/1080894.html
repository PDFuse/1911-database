Serial number 1080894 is a strong Union Switch & Signal M1911A1 example because both the frame and slide are identified as US&S. The pistol is not presented as an untouched factory-original example because the CMP data sheet documents RIA and ANAD 7-78 rebuild markings and a Colt replacement barrel.

Why This Pistol Matters

Union Switch & Signal produced far fewer M1911A1 pistols than the major wartime contractors, making US&S examples important to document individually. Serial number 1080894 is especially useful because the CMP data sheet records the frame, slide, barrel, rebuild markings, inspection mark, grip type, and bore condition.

The combination of US&S frame and US&S slide keeps the pistol highly desirable even though it is an arsenal rebuild. The RIA and ANAD 7-78 marks add postwar service history and help explain the Colt replacement barrel and military refinish.
