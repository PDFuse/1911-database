Why This Pistol Matters

Serial number 1935389 is an excellent example of a Remington Rand M1911A1 that remained in U.S. military service after World War II and later passed through Anniston Army Depot. The ANAD rebuild stamp tells an important service-history story that is different from an untouched original pistol.

For the 1911 Database, this pistol is valuable because it documents a legitimate arsenal-rebuilt service pistol with many correct and appropriate components: Remington Rand frame and slide, F.J.A. inspection, crossed-cannon ordnance acceptance, High Standard barrel, GI small parts, and arsenal parkerized finish.

Production and Historical Context

Production

Remington Rand was the largest WWII producer of M1911A1 pistols. Serial number 1935389 falls in the late wartime production period.

Inspector

F.J.A. refers to Frank J. Atwood, whose inspection initials are expected on Remington Rand and Ithaca M1911A1 pistols from this period.

ANAD Rebuild

The ANAD mark identifies Anniston Army Depot involvement. Arsenal rebuilds were official military refurbishments performed to keep pistols serviceable.

HS Barrel

High Standard barrels are correct WWII military barrels and are commonly encountered in Remington Rand production and later military rebuilds.
