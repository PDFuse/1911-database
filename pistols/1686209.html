CMP Notes

CMP Service Grade context: A CMP Service Grade pistol is evaluated and released by CMP based on its condition and serviceability category, not because every part is factory-original to the receiver. Mixed slides, replacement barrels, arsenal refinish, and rebuild markings are common on U.S. service pistols released through CMP.

What this example shows: The tag identifies a Colt frame with a Remington Rand slide. That combination should be understood as a U.S. service / rebuild configuration rather than a factory Colt slide-and-frame match.

Why ANAD 78 matters: ANAD indicates Anniston Army Depot. The “78” date mark points to 1978 processing or rebuild-era handling. That helps explain why a 1944 Colt frame may appear with a Remington Rand slide and later service finish characteristics.

Configuration Interpretation

Database classification: This should be listed as a Colt frame / Remington Rand slide CMP Service Grade rebuild.

Not original factory configuration: A factory-original Colt in this serial range would generally be expected to have a Colt slide and late-war Colt configuration. The Remington Rand slide and ANAD 78 marking point to later U.S. service rebuild or depot processing history.
