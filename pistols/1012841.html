Strong Early Remington Rand / Rough Machining Example

Serial number 1012841 is documented here as a 1943 Remington Rand M1911A1 with desirable early Type 2 slide markings and visible early-production machining character. The seller describes this pistol as 100% correct with original sandblasted Du-Lite blue finish.

The rough frame machining is the key educational feature of this record. Rather than treating it as damage, this page records it as a notable early wartime production characteristic associated with Remington Rand ramp-up manufacturing.

Why This Pistol Matters

This pistol is useful to document because it represents an early Remington Rand M1911A1 during wartime production ramp-up. The seller specifically identifies visibly rough frame machining contours as a notable feature, and the photographs show a pistol with early production character rather than the more uniform appearance often associated with later production.

For collectors, the combination of early Remington Rand markings, F.J.A. inspection, original Du-Lite finish claim, HS/P barrel claim, Keyes Fibre grip claim, L magazine, and Boyt holster makes this a strong research example if the seller's originality claims are verified.
