Original Configuration Guide

This section describes what a late-war Colt M1911A1 in the 2309527 serial range would generally be expected to show if it remained in original factory configuration. It is a comparison guide only, not a conclusion that this pistol is fully original.

FrameColt M1911A1 frame with UNITED STATES PROPERTY and M1911A1 U.S. ARMY markings appropriate to late 1945 Colt production.
Inspector MarkLate Colt production in this range should be reviewed for the correct final-inspection mark and crossed-cannon ordnance mark. Exact inspection details should be verified by close-up photos.
SlideColt slide with late-war Colt roll markings. A matching-period Colt slide would be expected for a factory-original example.
FinishLate-war parkerized military finish. Wear, arsenal refinish, or mixed finish can indicate later service use or rebuild history.
BarrelColt-correct late-war .45 barrel should be verified by chamber, lug, and underside markings.
GripsLate-war Coltwood-style brown plastic grips may be expected on many late Colt examples, though service replacements are common.
Small PartsLate-war M1911A1 configuration: short trigger, arched mainspring housing, A1 grip safety, checkered or serrated controls depending on production details and service history.
MagazineCorrect magazine should be evaluated separately. Magazines were commonly replaced during service.
