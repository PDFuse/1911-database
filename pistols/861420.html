Authentic 1918 Colt M1911 Frame / Refinished Service Pistol With Non-Factory Slide Marking Note

Serial number 861420 falls within 1918 Colt M1911 production. Current photographs document a Colt military frame, G.H.S. inspection mark, property marking, long trigger, flat mainspring housing, WWI-style grip safety, and Colt slide markings.

This pistol should not be cataloged as a 1943 Colt M1911A1. The seller description called it a 1943 G.H.D.-inspected M1911A1, but the serial number and physical features support 1918 Colt M1911 identification. The seller also noted that the right-side slide M1911A1 U.S. ARMY rollmark is non-factory.

Why This Pistol Matters

Serial number 861420 is important because it demonstrates how a genuine U.S. military Colt frame can be misidentified in a modern listing. The seller described the pistol as a 1943 Colt M1911A1, but the serial number and visible M1911 frame features identify it as a 1918 Colt M1911.

For the 1911 Database, this record is useful as an educational example: verify the serial range, frame shape, inspector initials, slide markings, finish, and barrel together before accepting a seller title or auction description.
