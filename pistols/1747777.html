Collector Classification

This pistol is best classified as a WWII Colt-frame M1911A1 with a Remington Rand replacement slide. Mixed slide and frame combinations are commonly encountered on arsenal rebuilds, depot-maintained pistols, postwar service pistols, and CMP-returned examples. This record should not be treated as an all-original Colt factory configuration.
