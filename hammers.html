M1911 & M1911A1 Hammer Reference

Hammer style is a collector-reference feature for comparing M1911 and M1911A1 pistols. This page is for historical identification and documentation only.

Common Hammer Styles

StyleCollector Notes
Wide SpurOften associated with earlier M1911 production.
Short SpurOften associated with M1911A1 production.
Checkered SurfacePattern, finish, and wear should be reviewed with the rest of the pistol.
Mixed Service PartMay be seen on pistols with arsenal or depot history.

Collector Review Points

AreaWhat To Review
ProfileCompare hammer profile to the model and production period.
Surface PatternReview checkering or surface texture as a documentation point.
FinishCompare finish and wear to other visible small parts.
Period ContextReview the part with serial range, maker, and service history.
Collector Note: A hammer should be documented as part of the overall historical picture. Review it with serial range, finish, markings, and arsenal history.
