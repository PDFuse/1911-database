Springfield Armory M1911 Reference

Springfield Armory produced U.S. military M1911 pistols before production was suspended during World War I. Use this page as a cautionary reference for Springfield serial ranges, markings, and the Colt/Springfield overlap concerns that should be checked before identifying a pistol.

Identification warning: A Springfield-range serial number is only the starting point. Confirm identification with Springfield-specific slide markings, receiver markings, ordnance/inspection marks, barrel markings, small parts, finish, and any later rebuild evidence.

Photo Documentation Checklist

Receiver

Serial number, United States Property marking, inspection marks, trigger guard marks, and any rebuild marks.

Slide

Springfield slide legend, right-side model marking, sight profile, finish, and proof/inspection context.

Barrel

Barrel chamber and lug markings, proof marks, finish, and wear consistency.

Small Parts

Hammer, trigger, grip safety, mainspring housing, magazine catch, grips, and magazine.

Collector Notes

Springfield Armory pistols should be reviewed conservatively. Serial range, slide marking, receiver marking, barrel, finish, and small parts must agree before making a strong identification claim.
