Inspector Initials by Serial Range

Inspector Initials Inspector Name Approx. Serial Range Commonly Seen On Notes
J.M.G. Major John M. Gilbert 1 – 23000 Early Colt M1911 Used on very early Colt military M1911 pistols.
R.C.D. Major Robert C. Downie 23001 – 85000 Colt and Springfield Armory Seen on early production pistols before W.G.P. became common.
W.G.P. Major Walter G. Penfield 85001 – 290000 Colt and Springfield Armory Common WWI-era acceptance mark.
G.H.D. Colonel Guy H. Drewry Approx. 700001 – late WWII WWII Colt Commonly associated with WWII Colt M1911A1 pistols.
F.J.A. Colonel Frank J. Atwood WWII contractor production Ithaca, Remington Rand, Union Switch & Signal Important for identifying Ithaca pistols in duplicated serial ranges.
R.C.D. LTC R.C. Downie WWII US&S production Union Switch & Signal Also associated with Union Switch & Signal inspection references.
J.K.C. LTC John K. Christmas S800001 – S800500 Singer Associated with Singer contract pistols.
H.S. LTC Harrison Shaler Remington Rand production Remington Rand Listed as a Remington Rand inspector reference.

Manufacturer Inspector Reference

Manufacturer Inspectors / Inspection Initials Notes
Colt J.M.G., R.C.D., W.G.P., G.H.D. Inspector marks vary heavily by production period.
Springfield Armory R.C.D., W.G.P. Used on early U.S. military M1911 production.
Ithaca F.J.A. F.J.A. is especially important on duplicated serial ranges.
Remington Rand F.J.A., H.S. Common WWII contractor inspection references.
Union Switch & Signal F.J.A., R.C.D. US&S pistols may reference Atwood or Downie inspection data.
Singer J.K.C. Singer contract pistols fall in the S800001–S800500 range.
Note: Inspector information should be used as a supporting identification aid, not as the only proof of originality. Always compare serial range, frame markings, slide markings, finish, proof marks, and rebuild markings together.
