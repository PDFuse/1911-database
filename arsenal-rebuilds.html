Arsenal and Depot History

Augusta Arsenal — AA

Located in Augusta, Georgia. Augusta Arsenal operated from the early 1800s until the mid-1950s. During World War II and afterward, the facility supported storage, inspection, repair, overhaul, and rebuilding of military equipment and small arms. On M1911 and M1911A1 pistols, an AA mark is generally interpreted as evidence of arsenal rebuild or military service processing rather than untouched original factory condition.

Springfield Armory — SA

Located in Springfield, Massachusetts. Springfield Armory operated as the primary U.S. government armory from 1777 until 1968. It manufactured and serviced military arms for generations. SA markings on M1911 and M1911A1 pistols may indicate inspection, rebuild, repair, or military service processing after original manufacture.

Rock Island Arsenal — RIA

Located on Arsenal Island between Rock Island, Illinois and Davenport, Iowa. Rock Island Arsenal was established in the 1860s and remains active today. RIA markings are commonly encountered on rebuilt or service-processed pistols. Collectors frequently associate RIA marks with arsenal overhaul, replacement parts, reparkerized finishes, and long military service history.

Ogden Arsenal — OG

Located near Ogden, Utah. Ogden Arsenal operated primarily during World War II and the postwar era. The arsenal handled storage, supply, inspection, and repair operations for military equipment. OG marks are useful collector references when documenting a pistol that passed through later military inspection or rebuild channels.

Anniston Army Depot — ANAD

Located in Anniston, Alabama. Anniston Army Depot was established during World War II and remains an active military maintenance facility. ANAD markings are generally associated with later depot rebuild or service processing rather than original WWII factory condition. Pistols marked ANAD may show mixed parts, arsenal finishes, and postwar maintenance history.

Letterkenny Army Depot — LEAD

Located near Chambersburg, Pennsylvania. Letterkenny Army Depot was established in 1942 and remains active. LEAD references are associated with postwar inspection, maintenance, storage, and rebuild programs. On collector pistols, LEAD markings may document continued U.S. military service long after WWII production.

Benicia Arsenal — BA

Located in Benicia, California. Benicia Arsenal operated from 1849 until the 1960s and served as an important western U.S. Army ordnance and supply facility. BA markings are less common than AA, SA, or RIA marks, but they remain relevant for documenting military inspection and service history.

Raritan Arsenal

Located in Edison, New Jersey. Raritan Arsenal operated during and after World War II as a major ordnance, supply, and storage facility. Some X-prefix reference ranges are associated with Raritan Arsenal processing. These references can help document post-production handling and military administrative history.

Tokyo Arsenal References

Tokyo Arsenal references are associated with postwar ordnance activity in Japan following World War II. Some X-prefix records connect pistols to overseas inspection, storage, or military administrative handling. These references are valuable because they may document occupation-era or postwar service history.

Collector Note: Arsenal and depot marks can be part of legitimate military service history. Describe the pistol accurately and review all features together.
