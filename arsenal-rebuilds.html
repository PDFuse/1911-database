U.S. M1911 & M1911A1 Arsenal Rebuild Reference

Arsenal and depot markings help document military service history. These marks may indicate inspection, repair, overhaul, or later service processing. This page is a historical collector reference only.

Depot marks can add useful military history to a pistol record.
