Arsenal and Depot History

Augusta Arsenal — AA

Augusta Arsenal in Georgia was an important U.S. Army storage, repair, and overhaul facility. On M1911 and M1911A1 pistols, an AA mark is generally treated as evidence of military service processing rather than original factory condition.

Springfield Armory — SA

Springfield Armory was one of the most significant U.S. government arms facilities. For collectors, SA markings may connect a pistol to government inspection, repair, or service work after original manufacture.

Rock Island Arsenal — RIA

Rock Island Arsenal in Illinois has a long history of U.S. military small-arms support. RIA markings are commonly reviewed as part of a pistol's service history, especially when mixed parts or non-factory finish are present.

Ogden Arsenal — OG

Ogden Arsenal in Utah supported inspection, storage, and service processing for U.S. military arms. OG marks are useful collector clues when evaluating whether a pistol passed through a military facility after original production.

Anniston Army Depot — ANAD

Anniston Army Depot in Alabama is associated with later U.S. military depot work. ANAD markings usually point to later service inspection, repair, or processing rather than WWII factory-original configuration.

Letterkenny Army Depot — LEAD

Letterkenny Army Depot in Pennsylvania is associated with postwar U.S. military maintenance and depot support. LEAD markings can help document a pistol's later military service life.

Benicia Arsenal — BA

Benicia Arsenal in California was a historic U.S. Army arsenal and supply facility. BA markings are less commonly discussed than AA, SA, or RIA but may still be relevant when documenting service history.

Raritan Arsenal

Raritan Arsenal in New Jersey is connected with some X-prefix reference ranges and post-production military handling. Pistols tied to these references should be reviewed for full service-history context.

Tokyo Arsenal References

Some X-prefix records reference ordnance work or handling in Tokyo after World War II. These references are important because they can document overseas military service or administrative processing.

Collector Note: Arsenal and depot marks can be part of legitimate military service history. Describe the pistol accurately and review all features together.
