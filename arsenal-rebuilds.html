U.S. M1911 & M1911A1 Arsenal Rebuild Reference

Arsenal rebuild marks help identify pistols that were repaired, refinished, or rebuilt during military service life. Rebuilt pistols are legitimate military pistols, but they are generally classified differently than untouched original examples.

Rebuilds Are Legitimate

Arsenal rebuilds are authentic military service pistols, not fakes.

Original vs Rebuild

Rebuilds are usually valued differently than untouched original pistols.

Mixmaster Parts

Many rebuilt pistols contain mixed manufacturers and replacement parts.

Major Arsenal Rebuild Marks

Mark Arsenal / Depot Meaning Collector Notes
AA Augusta Arsenal Arsenal rebuild or overhaul mark. Common rebuild marking encountered on WWII and postwar service pistols.
SA Springfield Armory Springfield rebuild or inspection mark. Often associated with refinished and rebuilt service pistols.
RIA Rock Island Arsenal Rock Island Arsenal rebuild mark. Frequently seen on rebuilt pistols with mixed parts.
OG Ogden Arsenal Ogden Arsenal rebuild mark. Often found on pistols rebuilt during or after WWII.
ANAD Anniston Army Depot Postwar rebuild or depot maintenance mark. Common on later-service pistols.
LEAD Letterkenny Army Depot Depot rebuild or inspection mark. Seen on postwar rebuilt pistols.
BA Benicia Arsenal Benicia Arsenal rebuild or inspection reference. Less common than AA, SA, or RIA rebuild marks.

How Rebuilds Affect Collector Value

Pistol Type Collector Interest Typical Characteristics
Untouched Original Highest collector interest Matching finish, correct parts, no rebuild markings.
Arsenal Rebuild Historically desirable but different category Rebuild marks, replacement parts, arsenal parkerizing.
Commercial Refinish Usually lower collector interest Buffed markings, incorrect finish, modern replacement parts.
Collector Note: A rebuilt pistol is not automatically bad or fake. Arsenal rebuilds are legitimate military pistols with real service history. The important factor is describing and identifying the pistol accurately.
