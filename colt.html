Colt M1911 & M1911A1 Serial Number Ranges

Colt was the original manufacturer of the U.S. military M1911 and M1911A1. This page contains Colt serial number production ranges and collector reference information.

WWI Production

Early Colt M1911 pistols used inspectors such as JMG, RCD, and WGP.

WWII Production

WWII Colt pistols are commonly associated with GHD inspection marks and parkerized finishes.

Collector Verification

Verify slide markings, frame markings, finish, barrel markings, and rebuild stamps together.
