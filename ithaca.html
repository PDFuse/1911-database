Common Characteristics

  • Parkerized finish
  • F.J.A. inspection mark on frame
  • Crossed cannons ordnance stamp
  • WWII contractor frame markings
  • Ithaca slide marking variations

Collector Notes

Ithaca serial numbers from 856405 through 916404 overlap with Colt production ranges. Proper identification should include: frame markings, slide markings, F.J.A. inspection marks, finish characteristics, and ordnance markings.
