Union Switch & Signal M1911A1 Reference

Union Switch & Signal produced some of the most collectible WWII M1911A1 pistols. US&S pistols are known for relatively limited production and strong collector interest.

Limited Production

US&S produced far fewer pistols than Colt or Remington Rand.

Collector Interest

Correct original US&S pistols are highly desirable among WWII collectors.

Verification

Carefully inspect slide markings, finish, and rebuild indicators.
