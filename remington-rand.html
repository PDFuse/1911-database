Common Characteristics

  • Parkerized finish
  • F.J.A. inspection mark on frame
  • Crossed cannons ordnance stamp
  • Type variations in slides and grips
  • WWII contractor frame markings

Collector Notes

Remington Rand pistols are among the most commonly encountered WWII M1911A1 pistols. Correct identification should include: serial range, slide type, finish, inspection marks, ordnance marks, and rebuild markings.
